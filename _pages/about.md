Research Interests:
======
My research focuses on bilingual language development in children and adults. I am particularly interested in the acquisition of morphosyntax in French and English learners and in identifying typical versus atypical language performance patterns in bilingual children.

Teaching Interests:
======
I hold a joint appointment in the Department of Linguistics and the Department of Modern Languages, Literatures and Cultures. I teach classes in linguistics as well as French language courses at both the intermediate and advanced levels.
